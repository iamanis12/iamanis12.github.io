Flagship Application

Selfcash — Personal Finance Manager

A clean, device-first Android application built to track everyday income, expenses, balances, and private backups.

Selfcash for Android

Active Release

Selfcash helps individuals maintain clear records of cash, bank accounts, receivables, payables, and pocket expenses without unnecessary complexity. All records stay locally on the user's device, with optional encrypted-in-transit backups to the user's personal Google Drive App Data folder.

  • Local Room Database
  • Google Drive AppData Sync
  • PDF Monthly Reports
  • App Lock & Biometrics
  • Receivable & Payable Ledger
Official App Page → Privacy Policy
Device-First Architecture

Financial records are stored privately in local Android storage, ensuring offline reliability and direct user ownership.

Scoped Google Drive Backup

Uses drive.appdata permission strictly to save and restore app-specific backup files without accessing personal Drive files.

Transparent Data Practices

Zero third-party ad trackers or data selling. Complete documentation is publicly available in the Selfcash Privacy Policy.
